Returns, repetition and the OCD-related question

How often does an animal return, where does it return, and how is repetition organized in time?

Placement returns and old-location visits are different events. These are software-derived descriptions of checking-related behavior. Repetition alone does not establish compulsion.

Every observed excursion segment

One row per animal. A line spans a tracked excursion segment; a circle marks an observed return to placement; a diamond marks the first old-location entry in that segment. Dashed lines indicate incomplete paths. Only the solid, complete trips enter the later-P1-trip count. Click an animal to inspect it.

What the current data can address

  • Frequency of observed returns and complete round trips.
  • Repeated trips reaching the former-goal area, after the first.
  • Duration of complete trips before returning to placement.
  • Timing across the 120-second test, alongside scanning and movement.

Later P1 trips are a lower-bound count across complete excursions. Within-trip boundary crossings are not counted as independent checks. A zero in this column does not establish absence of visits on incomplete paths.

What makes this relevant to OCD research?

Experimental checking research has examined return frequency, return speed and the places visited between returns. These provide useful measurement questions for this study; they do not transfer a model's validity automatically to a different task.

Szechtman et al., 2001 — experimental checking criteria

Excessiveness needs a justified comparison. Ritual structure, resistance to interruption, and persistence despite consequences need appropriate measurements or manipulations. Anxiety, doubt and relief are not measured here.

Neural measurements to link

Plan A: PV/WFA

Proposed primary endpoint: the animal-level fraction of sampled DMS PV-positive profiles associated with WFA-positive nets. Secondary measures: PV-positive profile density and WFA-positive net density, subject to assay feasibility.

Plan B: GFAP only if PV/WFA are unavailable

This requires its own endpoint and interpretation. It cannot substitute numerically for PV/WFA.

No matched histology values have been imported. SP1 brains were reported fixed; KAC animals were reported alive. Specimen history and per-animal matching still need documentation.

How to use these profiles

  1. Inspect each dimension and its tracking coverage.
  2. Compare animals within the same test, then within sex.
  3. Examine paired KAC changes in original units.
  4. Use blinded video scoring to validate visits and scanning.
  5. Freeze the behavioral endpoints before testing matched histology associations.

No composite “compulsivity” score, latent behavioral types or neural results are inferred. Multiple measures can be mathematically related: counts and percentages share events and denominators. A multivariate model needs a limited, justified feature set and enough complete animals.

Definitions, provenance and missing values

Final models: KAC best-320 and SP1 best-190. Body tracking is cleaned and smoothed; short accepted interpolations remain in derived values and are reported per animal. Unresolved gaps remain unknown. Scale uses the existing 172 cm analysis diameter; the new portal brief specifies 172.5 cm, pending reconciliation before portal visuals. P1 is training-derived in KAC conditional on camera correspondence, and assumed by reflection in SP1.

Color uses average tied ranks scaled to 0–100 within the selected reference. This is a descriptive display, not a normative score. Changing the displayed sex filter does not change the reference unless you select the same-sex reference. No missing values are imputed.
