Sex Divergence in Platform Checking & Micro-Scanning Dynamics

Placed on a hidden platform for 120 s, males stay put while females actively disembark to revisit the former training platform (P1), across standard (1.5 cm) and raised (3.0 cm) water depths. DeepLabCut tracking reveals this divergence originates before anyone leaves: females engage in significantly elevated head-scanning sweeps while still anchored on the platform.

Spatial Trajectory Analysis

Leavers Direct Search Toward Former Platform Location (P1)

During 5 days of acquisition training, the platform resided at P1 (~74 cm from the checking test platform). On Day 6, disembarking females spent 8.7% of their off-platform time within 20 cm of P1, compared to only 4.7% at matched control coordinates (Wilcoxon p = 0.018, 15 rats).

What Holds

  • Binary sex divergence is robust: Replicated across three separate tests in two experimental cohorts. Females disembark; males remain anchored.
  • Depth independence: The divergence is already prominent at standard water depth (KAC Day 6, 1.5 cm), ruling out water depth as an artifactual trigger.
  • Pre-departure micro-scanning: Head turn velocity and angular sweep rates are elevated in females before any departure occurs.
  • Directional navigation toward P1: Disembarking females prioritize the training platform quadrant over control locations.
  • High-precision tracking fidelity: Tracking agrees with 47 of 50 experimenter log entries, with untracked missing time under 1% for every group.

What to Hold Back

  • Water depth effect confounded: In KAC, departures doubled on Day 8 (median 2 → 4, p = 0.003), but the sex × depth interaction in the mixed model is not significant (p = 0.13), and depth is confounded with test order and camera calibration.
  • VTE-like lean-out trend: Head rotation before pulling back vs leaving is exploratory and does not survive Holm correction (SP1 p = 0.20, D6 p = 0.60, D8 p = 0.91).
  • SP1 coordinate assumption: In SP1, P1 coordinates were estimated by mirroring across the pool centre rather than direct calibrated training coordinates.
  • Reminder training unmodeled: Day 7 reminder training between Day 6 and Day 8 is not yet tracked with pose estimation.
