# The Portal: Return

One-page treatment · approved by user “Yes do it” · implementation begun 9 November 2026

**Concept.** Enter a pool, then follow the choices that the recordings make visible: remain, orient, leave, visit, return, repeat. The central question is “What makes a return become a pattern?” OCD-related checking supplies the research question, not a diagnosis applied to the animals. Individual profiles form the bridge between an arresting trajectory and a defensible analysis.

**Threshold.** A dark water surface and the question “Why do we check things again and again?” A visible “Enter” button and ordinary scrolling both work. Sound starts muted. The water is explicitly illustrative; the first animal path appears only after its identity and test are shown. No narration claims access to the rat's thoughts.

**The pool.** Begin overhead, with placement and former-goal areas clearly distinguished. An optional orbit view reveals scale; an optional low camera is labeled an illustrative viewpoint, not recorded rat vision. Use the brief's 172.5 cm diameter only after reconciling the camera/zone calibration. Unverified cues and platform dimensions are omitted. Offer the scientific 2D view throughout.

**KAC: one animal, two tests.** A selected rat's Day 6 and Day 8 paths occupy separate, synchronized panels. Scrubbing reveals departure, goal-area entry and return timing. A profile drawer connects scanning, movement, learning history and repetition. The reminder day appears between tests. The takeaway: animals can differ in whether they move, where they go, and how they repeat; these components need separate measures. Depth, test order and camera are visibly confounded.

**SP1: a separate history.** Replay the confirmed 120-second placement window, with one animal selected by default and all animals available. Distinguish original positions, disclosed processing, gaps and derived regions. Show no fabricated continuous trails through missing time. The takeaway: a similar-looking return pattern across cohorts is a descriptive comparison, not proof of a shared mechanism.

**AK1: the measurement changes.** Start with the verified 30-animal paired display across three center configurations. Mean software passages are 1.80, 3.63 and 5.23. Each animal remains linked across settings; there are 90 representations, not 90 animals. No animated AK1 trajectory is shown without coordinates. Use discrete configurations, not a continuous geometry slider that pretends unknown zone sizes are known. The takeaway: changing a definition changes a count; it does not diagnose compulsion.

**Returns and repetition.** Bring frequency, destination and timing together. Keep placement returns separate from former-goal visits. For KAC/SP1, an exploratory zone/dwell control can recompute explicitly software-derived area entries from real positions after the pipeline is approved. Default: current 20 cm neighborhood, no minimum dwell. Show threshold sensitivity and boundary jitter rather than calling every crossing a validated check.

**The question beneath.** A restrained, labeled hypothesis panel links the behavioral profile to the planned animal-level PV/WFA endpoint. No histology is shown as a result. GFAP remains Plan B only if PV/WFA is unavailable. End with open validations, credits, and a “View the data” drawer containing source, animal, window, frame/time, processing and definition.

**Sound and motion.** Optional synthesized water ambience with a clear mute control. Any event-linked tone is labeled sonification, never a physiological heartbeat or emotional state. Reduced motion removes descent and camera movement. Every view has keyboard controls and a text/table equivalent.

**Build plan.** A static, mobile-first site, initially 2D; lazy-loaded Three.js enhancement only after geometry is supported. Reproducible Python extraction, immutable source hashes and compact per-cohort track payloads. Aim for under 25 MB total and under 4 seconds to interaction; benchmark, do not claim these targets as achieved. First show three raw tracked trajectories with QC overlays before visual treatment. Then publish protected stages: one cohort, remaining supported sections, measurement interaction, polish/accessibility. Real-phone testing and derivative video/poster assets follow later approval.
