Excursions have structure

Individual routes from leaving through sustained return, with incomplete segments retained.

An off-platform run must last ≥1 s; return requires ≥1 s on-platform. Unknown gaps split and censor segments. Dashed cards are incomplete and must not be counted as additional confirmed departures. What these visuals measure

Spatial occupancy, movement speed, on-platform head rotation, temporal sequences, observed departures, and sustained-return excursions. Sex is a comparison axis, not a behavioral category.

Where uncertainty stays visible

Tracking gaps remain gaps. Platform occupancy has lower and upper bounds. P1 geometry remains conditional. Display interpolation and complete-excursion selection are disclosed.

How to read group differences

Animals are the independent units. Events are repeated observations within animals. These displays are descriptive; no new significance tests or automatic psychological classifications are implied.
