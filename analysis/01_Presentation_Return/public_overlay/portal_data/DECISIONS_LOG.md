## Version 2 additions

- User approved the treatment with “Yes do it.” Build and publish the protected portal within the already authorized Vercel project and audience.
- Preserve newer local dashboard art and motion-lab files. This portal adds to the last verified 35-file protected deployment; it does not replace those local edits or silently publish unreviewed assets.
- Export native coordinates losslessly as float64 plus explicit quality flags. Retain 250,800 frames across 91 sessions. No resampling or extra interpolation.
- Original/unsmoothed view means the cached confidence-gated spine-mid/head-center body selection, not an untouched recording. Processed view visibly distinguishes short filled intervals. Long gaps are never connected.
- KAC original HDF paths were unavailable during this build. Local caches and provenance hashes are retained and verified. SP1 F25 was independently matched against its original HDF and source hash. Do not claim all original files were re-read.
- Exploratory area controls compute off-platform bouts and observed entries from processed native frames. Default 20 cm, no dwell. A transition after a gap has unknown onset; boundary jitter can inflate counts. These controls do not change the fixed complete-trip metrics.
- Initial release remains overhead 2D because scale/zone registration is unresolved. Animated water is illustrative. Sound is synthesized ambience, opt-in, never a physiological signal.
- Three pre-treatment QC trajectories are KAC F25 Day 6/8 and SP1 F25; SP1 F25 is a different animal. Selected for a paired moving example and a stationary example, not as cohort averages.

## Validation priorities before publication

1. Register the actual trained-goal and placement geometry to each camera; reconcile 172 versus 172.5 cm and the reference AnyMaze configuration.
2. Independently annotate a blinded sample of departures, both return destinations and head sweeps, including difficult tracks and brief area entries. Quantify agreement and error.
3. Confirm AK1 `49-Prb4` is the intended endpoint and recover the physical meaning of each center configuration. Keep the present comparison explicitly candidate/software-derived until then.
4. Reconcile SP1 daily acquisition row counts and `d6/d6rev` representations with raw exports, retaining the confirmed sex roster and 120-second window.
5. Separate true stillness/floating from tracking/scale differences before any speed interpretation across cohorts. Document HVS problems rather than correcting by assumption.
6. Assess whether behavioral endpoints can support an “excessive” or “persistent despite consequences” claim. Neither a high count nor a high rank establishes this alone.
7. Document matched tissue identities, fixation/storage, intervening exposures and assay pilot quality before histology associations.

No final portal geometry or full publication-level validation is claimed by this log.
