Platform Checking Study

Placed on the hidden platform for 120 s, most males stay put and most females leave, in an earlier cohort with raised water and in a second cohort at both standard and raised depth. Pose tracking shows the difference starts before anyone leaves: females scan with their heads far more while still on the platform.

What holds

  • The no-observed-departure classification differs by sex in all three tests. Four zero-event sessions contain missing time and need video resolution before being called confirmed stayers.
  • It is already there at standard depth (KAC Day 6), so raised water is not what makes females leave.
  • Head scanning on the platform is higher in females in all three tests.
  • Females who leave head toward where the platform used to be (P1) more than toward control spots (Day 6).
  • KAC platform time agrees closely with AnyMaze. Binary stayed/left agrees with 18/20 explicit Day 6 notes and 29/30 Day 8 notes; the other three comparisons remain unknown after cleaning.

What to hold back

  • Standard vs raised water in the same rats: females 71% on Day 6 vs 63% on Day 8, and their departures doubled (median 2 → 4, p = 0.003). But in the mixed model the water effect is not significant (sex × water p = 0.13), and depth is confounded with test order and a camera change.
  • VTE-like pattern (more head rotation before pulling back than before leaving): Holm p = 0.20 (SP1), 0.60 (Day 6), 0.91 (Day 8). A trend, not a finding yet.
  • P1 assumes the camera did not move between training and testing; SP1's P1 is assumed from the opposite quadrant.
  • SP1 sex labels were verified against the original weight workbook on 8 November: rats 1–16 male, 17–32 female. Day 7 reminder training is not tracked.
  • Platform placement is in the opposite quadrant from the learned location. Learned-place visits and reentries, the thesis's primary behavioral measure, still require a separate region and validated event analysis.
Where they go when they leave

Leavers head toward where the platform used to be

During training the platform sat at P1, about 74 cm from the checking-test platform. On Day 6, females that left spent 8.7% of their off-platform time within 20 cm of P1, against 4.7% at matched control spots (Wilcoxon p = 0.018, 15 rats). P1 is marked on the KAC path maps and demos (purple diamond and dotted ring). It assumes the camera was in the same position as in training; In SP1 the training platform was in the opposite quadrant, so P1 is placed by mirroring the checking platform through the pool centre (assumed, not measured).

SP1 shows the same direction but is not significant: leavers spent 7.5% of off-platform time within 20 cm of P1 vs 3.9% at control spots (Wilcoxon p = 0.16, 16 rats). Of the departures that ended back on the platform, 11 of 29 on KAC Day 6 and 15 of 29 in SP1 passed within 20 cm of P1 without reaching the wall.
